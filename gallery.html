Apps for Nature ↻ Surprise me

Apps made by Year 7, for nature

Every app here was designed and built by a Year 7 student as part of the school tree project. They help you identify leaves and minibeasts, and work out how old and how tall a tree is. Pick one to try, then tell its maker what you think.

Apps are checked by staff before they are published and contain no personal information. Feedback is shared with the students anonymously.
